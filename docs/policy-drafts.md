## Terms of Service

**Title:** `Terms of Service`
**Version:** `v1.0`

Accommo is a boarding-house listing and tenancy platform operated by
{{ operating office — e.g. the ISU Echague Office of Student Affairs and
Services }} for students of {{ institution }} and the accommodation managers who
list with it. By creating an account you agree to these terms.

**Who may use Accommo.** Student accounts are for enrolled students of
{{ institution }}. Accommodation manager accounts are for people who own or are
authorised to manage the accommodation they list. One person, one account. You
are responsible for everything done under your account, including keeping your
password and device secure.

**Verification.** Both roles are verified before they gain full access. Students
submit a school ID and assessment of fees; accommodation managers submit their
sanitary permit, fire safety certificate, business permit and building permit.
Documents are reviewed by {{ operating office }} staff, who may approve, reject,
or ask for a resubmission. Accounts can be suspended if a document is found to
be false, expired, or belonging to someone else.

**Listings and bookings.** Accommodation managers are responsible for the
accuracy of everything they publish: rooms, capacity, rent, deposits, house
rules, photos, and availability. {{ operating office }} verifies documents and
accreditation status, but does not inspect every room, guarantee any listing, or
act as a party to the tenancy. The lease is between the student and the
accommodation manager. Rent, deposits, and advances are collected and recorded
outside Accommo; entries in the app are a record, not a payment service.

**Conduct.** Do not post false, misleading, discriminatory, or harassing
content; do not upload documents that are not yours; do not use another
person's account; do not attempt to access data belonging to other users. Report
problems through the support tickets or concerns screens.

**Content you submit.** You keep ownership of the photos, documents, reviews,
and messages you upload. You grant {{ operating office }} permission to store
and display them within Accommo for the purpose of running the platform.

**Suspension and removal.** Accounts, listings, and content that breach these
terms may be suspended or removed. Where the breach involves a student's
conduct in an accommodation, {{ operating office }} may refer the matter through
the usual student-discipline process.

**Availability.** Accommo is provided as-is. Service may be interrupted for
maintenance or for reasons outside our control, and {{ operating office }} is
not liable for losses arising from an interruption, a listing's inaccuracy, or a
dispute between a student and an accommodation manager.

**Changes.** These terms may be updated. When a newer version takes effect you
will be asked to accept it the next time you open the app. Continuing to use
Accommo after that point means you accept the current version.

**Contact.** {{ office contact — email, phone, physical office }}

## Privacy Policy

**Title:** `Privacy Policy`
**Version:** `v1.0`

This policy explains what Accommo collects, why, and who can see it. It is
written to align with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173);
confirm the details with your Data Protection Officer.

**What we collect.** Account details (name, sex, email address, mobile number,
profile photo, and for students the student number, college, program and year
level). Verification documents (school ID, assessment of fees, and for
accommodation managers the permits listed in the Terms of Service). Tenancy
records (leases, room assignments, payment entries, boarding history). Content
you create (messages, support tickets, concerns, reviews, listing photos).
Technical data needed to run the app (sign-in times, device push token, and
approximate map location when you search for accommodation nearby).

**Why we collect it.** To verify that students are enrolled and that
accommodation is accredited; to connect students with accommodation managers; to
run leases, payments records, and support; and to let {{ operating office }}
oversee accredited accommodation.

**Who can see what.** Accommodation managers see the profile and tenancy details
of students who apply to or occupy their rooms. Students see the manager's
public profile and listing details. {{ operating office }} staff can see
accounts, documents, verification requests, tickets, and audit records. Other
students cannot see your documents, messages, or tenancy details. Access is
enforced in the database itself, not only in the app.

**Where it is stored.** Account and tenancy data are stored with Supabase;
uploaded images are stored with Cloudinary; maps are served by Mapbox. These
providers process data on our behalf and may store it outside the Philippines.

**How long we keep it.** Account and tenancy records are kept while the account
is active and for {{ retention period }} afterwards, so that boarding history
and disputes can be settled. Verification documents are kept for
{{ retention period }} after a decision.

**Your rights.** You may view and correct your profile in the app, ask for a
copy of your data, object to processing, or ask for deletion. Deletion requests
that conflict with an active lease or an open case may be deferred until the
matter closes. Contact {{ Data Protection Officer contact }}.

**Security.** Passwords are hashed and never visible to staff. Documents are
kept in restricted storage. Staff actions on accounts and verifications are
recorded in an audit log.

**Changes.** When this policy is updated you will be asked to accept the new
version the next time you open the app.
